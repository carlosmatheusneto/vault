### Two ways to model charge

- **Concentrated (point charge):** a single value $q$ at a point.
- **Distributed:** spread over a volume, surface, or line → described by a **density**.

### Charge densities

Each density is defined as a limit (charge per unit dimension), which makes it a **function of position**:

Volumetric  $\rho_V$ 
	$Q=\iiint_V \rho_V,dv$

Surface $\rho_S$
	$Q=\iint_S \rho_S,ds$

Line $\rho_L$
	$Q=\int_L \rho_L,dl$
