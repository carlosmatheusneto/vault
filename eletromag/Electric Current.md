
### Volumetric current density $\vec{J}$

For current spread through a **volume** (a "current tube," e.g. a solid wire):

$$J = \lim_{\Delta s \to 0}\frac{\Delta I}{\Delta s} = \frac{dI}{ds} \quad(\Delta s \perp \text{current})$$

$$I = \iint_\varsigma \vec{J}\cdot d\vec{s}$$
### Surface current density $\vec{J}_S$

For current flowing on a **thin sheet** (a "current sheet," e.g. a hollow cylinder's shell):

$$J_S = \lim_{\Delta l \to 0}\frac{\Delta I}{\Delta l} = \frac{dI}{dl} \quad(\Delta l \perp \text{current, on the sheet})$$

$$dI = \vec{J}_S\cdot\hat{n},dl \quad\Longrightarrow\quad I = \int_\xi \vec{J}_S\cdot\hat{n},dl$$
